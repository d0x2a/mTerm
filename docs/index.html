Kuddo

The same terminal under a name of its own, since “mterm” already belonged to several other terminals.

Kuddo

Go to Kuddo

Kuddo 1.0.0 picked up where mTerm 1.6.0 left off. On Apple silicon, a Homebrew install of mTerm moves across on the next brew upgrade, and the first launch brings your settings, profiles, triggers, themes and saved tabs with it.

On an Intel Mac

Kuddo is built for Apple silicon only. mTerm 1.6.0 is the last release with an Intel build: mTerm-1.6.0.dmg.

A universal binary, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Requires macOS 14 or later.

mTerm’s source, history and releases stay in the archived repository at github.com/d0x2a/mTerm.
